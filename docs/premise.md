# Project Tango Charlie premise

The pilot is lost or stranded on an unfamiliar planet with their trusted
transformable battledroid, TC167. The pilot's gender is intentionally unspecified.

Tango Charlie is the fictional company/organization associated with TC167.

TC167 has three configurations:

- FIGHTER: high-speed aircraft configuration.
- VTOL: intermediate vertical-takeoff/hover configuration.
- BATTLEDROID: humanoid combat configuration.
