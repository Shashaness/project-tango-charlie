# Protonic energy architecture

Fusion generates energy. Protonic batteries store it. The fictional storage and charging physics remain open.

A battery definition has an equipment interface and a condition. Its state also tracks a normalized charge from zero to one. Conditions are serviceable, deteriorated, failed, and unknown. A serviceable battery can be depleted. Equipment can require an interface, a serviceable battery, and a configurable minimum charge.

The Q1.3A ATAT-773 profile requires the industrial interface at a provisional minimum charge of 25%. Its original battery is serviceable and empty. Hangar C initializes battery A at 62% and serviceable and battery B at 13% and deteriorated. Neither the station's operation nor the duration of surviving charge is established.

Mechanical towability is independent of power. Installing a compatible charged battery makes recovery controls available. Loading stabilization, securing an aircraft, and loaded-transport readiness remain separate states for later checkpoints. Helmet depletion and energy consumption are deferred.
