# Outstanding mysteries

All questions below are **UNRESOLVED**. The supporting observations establish the questions and leave their answers open.

| Question | Established context |
| --- | --- |
| What sustains Earth's remaining atmospheric oxygen? | The atmosphere retains enough oxygen to be somewhat breathable. [Earth](locations/earth.md) |
| Why are earthquakes and volcanic activity becoming more frequent? | Both are increasing. [Earth](locations/earth.md) |
| Was Earth completely abandoned? | Humanity dispersed in an exodus. Complete abandonment is a widely held belief. [Human exodus](history/human_exodus.md) |
| Do other humans or intelligent entities remain on Earth? | Kara believes she is alone. [Kara Voss](characters/kara_voss.md) |
| Does abandoned infrastructure still operate? | Kara's objectives include establishing survival infrastructure. The condition of earlier infrastructure remains open. |
| How accurate is humanity's understanding of the intergalactic war? | Humanity participated in the war. Its precise origins, participants, chronology, and political circumstances remain unestablished. [Intergalactic war](history/intergalactic_war.md) |
| What was the unexplained signal? | The signal remains outstanding. Its origin, sender, timing, and meaning remain open. [Initial world state](world/initial_world_state.md) |
| Can Earth actually be restored? | Testing the feasibility of restoration is a mission objective. [Canon](CANON.md) |

The [Chronicles](chronicles/README.md) and [Kara's journal](journals/kara_voss/README.md) contain proposed details relevant to these questions. Any resolution requires explicit approval.

## Protonic Age questions

The exact current terrestrial year, the storage mechanism and charging requirements of protonic batteries, and the reason a service-station battery retains charge remain open. The historical interlude leaves the transmission mystery unresolved. [Protonic Age](history/protonic_age.md)
