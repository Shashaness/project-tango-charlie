# Project Tango Charlie
## The Return to Earth

> Humanity's greatest mission is no longer to conquer the stars, but to bring life back to the world it left behind.

Humanity damaged its ancestral home, scattered among the stars, and found itself unwelcome among other civilizations. Generations later, Commander Kara Voss returns to an Earth she knows primarily through historical records. Her mission is to restore a living world and give humanity a second chance.

## Review and approval

1. Record new material as **PROPOSED**. Identify the records it would affect and any gaps it would close. Mark a character's interpretation as **IN-UNIVERSE BELIEF** and attribute it to the holder.
2. Review the proposal against [Canon](CANON.md) and [Mysteries](MYSTERIES.md). Flag continuity conflicts and any new information that would reveal an unresolved answer.
3. Obtain explicit project-owner approval before adding or revising **CANON**. Update the authoritative record, then reconcile the relevant timeline, glossary, and linked references. Record the approved change and its review reference in the canon record.
4. Apply the same review to later corrections. Draft prose, journal entries, and simulation records require their own publication review.

## Future narrative architecture

This directory provides the approved historical and fictional foundations for gameplay and future narrative work. Persistent autonomous characters will have individual memories, responsibilities, goals, and relationships. Each character's knowledge will be scoped to what they have learned, observed, or could reasonably acquire.

Future simulation events can produce living chronicles, mission reports, construction histories, scientific discoveries, and personal journals. These records must cite or otherwise trace to actual events. AI-generated narrative may describe events within established canon. Changes to canon require owner approval through the same editorial process.

The future **PTC Chronicles** website and digital book will publish approved material and suitable persistent-world records. Its planned collection includes sequential chapters, biographies, historical archives, colony histories, technology references, personal journals, mission reports, construction and restoration milestones, and scientific discoveries. Public visibility requires editorial review, particularly for private character knowledge, unpublished drafts, and material that could disclose unresolved mysteries.

## Literary direction

Use mature, atmospheric science fiction with a sense of isolation and discovery. Ground scientific and technical detail where the setting supports it. Give characters emotional depth through observation, choices, and consequences. Reveal historical and scientific mysteries gradually. Keep exposition economical, use established terms, and distinguish what a character perceives from what the setting establishes. Humanity's redemption through planetary restoration anchors the story.
