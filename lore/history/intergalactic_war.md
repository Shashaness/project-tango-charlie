# The intergalactic war

## Canon

Humanity participated in a catastrophic intergalactic war. In the aftermath it became fractured and scattered, and other intelligent civilizations shunned it. The [relative timeline](../TIMELINE.md) places the war before the [human exodus](human_exodus.md).

## Unresolved

The war's origins, participants beyond humanity, precise chronology, and political circumstances remain open. [Earth's](../locations/earth.md) devastation involved environmental abuse and warfare. The exact relationship between the war and individual events on Earth remains unestablished.

## In-universe belief

Humanity's own historical understanding may be incomplete or inaccurate. Individual accounts can be attributed to their holders when developed.

## Proposed

The [prologue](../chronicles/000_prologue.md) frames humanity's ambition and conquest as a prelude to the war. This causal framing awaits approval.
