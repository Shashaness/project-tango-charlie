# The Protonic Age

**Canon.** In 2243 humanity mastered controlled fusion and advanced energy storage. Compact systems used water as their principal feedstock and small amounts of consumable chemical compounds. Domestic installations promised extraordinary abundance. Central and its allied interests monopolized generation and criminalized unlicensed use. Convictions could mean lifelong labor in distant mining colonies.

Fusion, storage, and AI-accelerated science enabled interstellar expansion. Humanity encountered other civilizations and chose conquest. As the First Intergalactic War grew, Earth supplied seawater to a war effort spanning a galaxy where water was comparatively scarce. Oceans began receding over centuries of warfare and environmental exploitation. A coalition eventually defeated humanity's empire. Dispersed survivors included colonies that had opposed conquest and sought peaceful coexistence.

Nearly a millennium after the downfall, the uncertain terrestrial calendar is believed to be in the 3000s. Kara's forward operation is assessing Earth's habitability and prospects for restoration. Her immediate priority is recovering TC167. [The Price of Infinity](../chronicles/the_price_of_infinity.md)
