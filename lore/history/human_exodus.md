# The human exodus

## Canon

Humanity departed [Earth](../locations/earth.md) and dispersed into space. Human colonies persisted independently of Earth. One colony undertook the [return expedition](../colonies/README.md) to investigate whether humans could survive on Earth again.

## In-universe belief

Earth is generally believed to have been abandoned. [Kara Voss](../characters/kara_voss.md) believes she is alone. Whether anyone remained or returned independently is [unresolved](../MYSTERIES.md).

## Proposed and pending

The [prologue](../chronicles/000_prologue.md) describes great colony vessels carrying the remnants of human civilization and later generations learning of Earth through records and stories. The details of those vessels, the exodus, and individual colonies await review.
