# Relative timeline

The sequence gives historical waypoints. The environmental damage accumulated over time, and the ordering leaves room for overlap. The 2243 breakthrough is dated. The other dates remain approximate or unknown.

1. In 2243 humanity masters controlled fusion and advanced storage. Monopoly and AI-accelerated interstellar expansion follow. [Protonic Age](history/protonic_age.md)
2. Humanity pursues conquest. The First [Intergalactic War](history/intergalactic_war.md) drains Earth’s oceans, and a coalition eventually defeats the empire.
3. Environmental abuse and wartime damage culminate in the devastation of [Earth](locations/earth.md).
4. Humanity disperses into space during the [human exodus](history/human_exodus.md).
5. [Human colonies](colonies/README.md) continue independently of Earth.
6. Nearly a millennium later, in an uncertain year believed to be in the 3000s, one colony undertakes a deep-space return expedition to investigate whether humans could survive on Earth again.
7. [Commander Kara Voss](characters/kara_voss.md) enters Earth's atmosphere.
8. During uncontrolled descent, Kara spots a city and attempts to reach an airport near it. She loses consciousness before [TC167](technology/tc167.md) crashes near the airport. She survives, and TC167 is severely damaged.
9. Kara awakens, gets out of the wreckage, and starts toward the maintenance hangar. Her restoration mission begins with the need to recover TC167. [Chapter II](chronicles/002_the_arrival.md) · [Initial world state](world/initial_world_state.md)

## Intended campaign sequence

Kara investigates the outlying service compound, discovers the Jeep, and uses it to reach the airport. She searches the maintenance hangar, finds the key, and accesses the heavy recovery facility. The sequence describes intended progress. Specific observations and journal entries depend on the player's actions.
