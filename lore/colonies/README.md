# Human colonies

## Canon

Human colonies continued independently of Earth after the [exodus](../history/human_exodus.md). One unnamed colony undertook a deep-space journey toward Earth to investigate whether humans could survive there again. [Commander Kara Voss](../characters/kara_voss.md) originates from the colony that dispatched her.

## Pending development

Names, locations, populations, political systems, technologies, and colony histories await approval. The [first chapter](../chronicles/001_the_return.md) proposes that the mission was unprecedented in Kara's colony.
