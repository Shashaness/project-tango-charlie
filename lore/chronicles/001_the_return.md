# Chapter I — Commander Kara Voss

Commander Kara Voss had never seen an ocean.

She had never stood beneath a tree, felt rain against her skin, or watched a living forest move in the wind.

Earth was a place she knew only through the archives of her colony.

And yet, as her fighter approached the planet, she felt something she could not entirely explain.

She was coming home.

Her mission was unprecedented in the history of her colony: return to humanity's ancestral world, establish a permanent operating base, assess the planet's habitability, and determine whether Earth could one day sustain a living civilization again.

It was not a mission of conquest.

It was a mission of restoration.

Her assigned spacecraft, tactical fighter TC167, had been adapted for atmospheric reconnaissance and independent surface operations.

But atmospheric entry did not go according to plan.

Violent turbulence and cascading system failures left the aircraft damaged. Fighting to maintain control, Kara struggled to reach the surface of a planet that had not welcomed her kind for generations. As the ground rushed toward her, consciousness slipped away.

She survived.

Her aircraft would need repairs. Her supplies were limited. Her mission had scarcely begun.

Standing beneath the unfamiliar sky, looking across the barren landscape of humanity's birthplace, Kara understood the magnitude of what lay ahead.

She would need shelter. Power. Resources. A means of communication.

She would need to build.

She believed she was the first human to return to Earth in generations.

She believed that everyone had left.

She believed she was alone.

She was wrong.
