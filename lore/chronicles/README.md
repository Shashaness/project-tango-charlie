# The PTC Chronicles

The opening sequence of **Project Tango Charlie — The Return to Earth** is under editorial review.

1. [Prologue — The World We Left Behind](000_prologue.md) covers humanity's fall, Earth's devastation, and departure. Proposed details include conquest as a cause of war, the specific fate of oceans, forests and cities, surviving scientific records, volcanic dormancy, and the colony vessels.
2. [Chapter I — Commander Kara Voss](001_the_return.md) follows Kara's arrival. Proposed details include her prior experiences, the colony's mission precedent, TC167's adaptations, the entry failures and landing, and her resources and immediate needs. The closing line is proposed dramatic framing. Whether Kara is alone, and whether she is the first to return, remain open.

3. [Chapter II — The Arrival](002_the_arrival.md) depicts the descent, crash, and Kara's departure toward the maintenance hangar.

The prologue and Chapter I remain proposed. The owner approved Chapter II as canon during Q1.2 review, along with a targeted consistency edit to Chapter I. See the [approval record](../CANON.md#approval-record). Use [Canon](../CANON.md) for objective facts and the [mystery register](../MYSTERIES.md) for unanswered questions.
