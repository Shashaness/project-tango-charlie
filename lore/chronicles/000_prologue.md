# PROJECT TANGO CHARLIE
## The Return to Earth
### Prologue — The World We Left Behind

Once, humanity looked toward the stars with wonder.

In time, that wonder became ambition. Ambition became conquest. And conquest brought a war whose consequences would reach far beyond the boundaries of human civilization.

The intergalactic war left humanity fractured, scattered, and shunned by the very civilizations it had once sought to join.

Earth, the birthplace of humanity, suffered most of all.

Centuries of exploitation, followed by nuclear, chemical, and biological warfare, had transformed a once vibrant planet into an immense desert wasteland. Oceans had largely disappeared. Forests had turned to dust. Cities that once housed millions stood silent beneath the shifting sands.

From orbit, Earth was scarcely distinguishable from Mars.

And yet, inexplicably, the planet had not entirely died.

Its atmosphere remained. Its gravity held. Traces of oxygen persisted, enough to sustain human life under favorable conditions, though no surviving scientific record could adequately explain their continued presence.

Beneath the surface, the planet was becoming increasingly unstable. Earthquakes grew more frequent. Volcanoes awakened after centuries of silence.

Something was changing.

Humanity had long since departed, carrying what remained of its civilization aboard great colony vessels, searching the darkness for somewhere to begin again.

Generations passed.

For those born among the stars, Earth became little more than a collection of historical records, fragmented images, and stories told by the descendants of those who had once walked upon its soil.

But not everyone had forgotten.
