# Canon

This is the authoritative record of approved facts for **Project Tango Charlie — The Return to Earth**.

| Classification | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **CANON** | Facts explicitly approved by the project owner. |
| **PROPOSED** | Narrative material awaiting owner approval. |
| **UNRESOLVED** | Questions intentionally left unanswered. |
| **IN-UNIVERSE BELIEF** | A belief or account held by a character or civilization. Approval can establish who holds it while its accuracy remains open. |

## Approved facts

- Humanity participated in a catastrophic intergalactic war. In its aftermath, humanity became fractured, scattered, and shunned by other intelligent civilizations. [Intergalactic war](history/intergalactic_war.md)
- Centuries of environmental abuse and nuclear, chemical, and biological warfare left Earth a largely uninhabitable desert with a Mars-like surface. Its atmosphere and substantially intact gravity remain. Residual oxygen makes the atmosphere somewhat breathable. Earthquakes and volcanic activity are becoming more frequent. [Earth](locations/earth.md)
- Humanity dispersed into space in an exodus. Human colonies continued independently of Earth. One colony undertook a deep-space journey toward Earth to investigate whether humans could survive there again. [Human exodus](history/human_exodus.md) · [Colonies](colonies/README.md)
- Commander Kara Voss, the first canonical character, comes from a human colony that departed Earth long ago. She was dispatched to Earth to establish a permanent operating base, assess habitability and environmental conditions, build infrastructure for long-term survival, and determine whether Earth can be terraformed and restored to support civilization. Restoration is her mission. [Kara Voss](characters/kara_voss.md)
- **TC167** is Kara's assigned fighter. It sustained damage during atmospheric entry. Kara survived, and the aircraft requires repairs. [TC167](technology/tc167.md)

- Kara's Terrestrial Navigation Helmet reports position, heading, and elevation independently of TC167. Its rechargeable micro protonic battery supports several days of continuous navigation. [Terrestrial Navigation Helmet](technology/terrestrial_navigation_helmet.md)

## Q1 additions

During her uncontrolled descent, Kara spotted a city and attempted to reach an airport near it. She lost consciousness before TC167 crashed near the airport and survived. TC167 is severely damaged and may be beyond repair. Kara must reach the maintenance hangar and recover her aircraft. The Jeep is in an outlying service compound near the crash site, and recovery equipment is at the airport. The unexplained signal remains an outstanding mystery. [Initial world state](world/initial_world_state.md)

## Q1.2A additions

An [outlying service compound](locations/outlying_service_compound.md) stands near the crash site and contains the Jeep. In the intended sequence Kara uses it to reach the airport. The airport has at least two hangars. The [maintenance hangar](locations/maintenance_hangar.md) contains an office, and a key concealed inside a desk drawer grants personnel access to the [heavy recovery hangar](locations/heavy_recovery_hangar.md). The second hangar houses stationary lifting equipment and a towable recovery trolley.

## In-universe beliefs

Earth is generally believed to have been abandoned. Kara believes humanity left and that she is alone. The exodus is established. Its completeness and the identity or existence of anyone still on Earth remain [unresolved](MYSTERIES.md). Humanity's accounts of the war may be incomplete or inaccurate.
