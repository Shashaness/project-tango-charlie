# Protonic batteries

Protonic batteries store energy for personal electronics, vehicles, industrial equipment, aircraft, spacecraft, and infrastructure. Compatibility depends on the equipment and the battery type. An industrial battery used by ATAT-773 and the helmet's micro battery serve different applications.

Stored charge and physical condition are separate. A serviceable battery may be depleted. The cause of the usable charge found at the service station and the station's operating history remain unknown. [ATAT-773](atat_773.md) · [Helmet](terrestrial_navigation_helmet.md)
