# Utility Jeep

A Jeep is available within the [outlying service compound](../locations/outlying_service_compound.md). Its precise location becomes Kara's knowledge once she discovers it. The vehicle is accessible and apparently operational in the Q001 scenario. Entering and driving it confirms that it can move under its own power.

The manufacturer, age, original owner, and reason it still works remain unestablished. [Q001 — Remnants of Civilization](../missions/Q001_remnants_of_civilization.md)
