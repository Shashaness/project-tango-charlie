# Terrestrial Navigation Helmet

**Classification:** CANON  
**Technology category:** Personal equipment  
**Current operator:** Commander Kara Voss

The flight helmet worn by Commander Kara Voss incorporates a compact, independent electronic navigation system designed to remain operational when the pilot is separated from her aircraft.

Its integrated heads-up display provides essential terrestrial navigation information, including geographical position, elevation, and directional heading.

Unlike the more sophisticated avionics aboard TC167, the helmet's navigation electronics operate independently of aircraft power and communications systems.

The electronics are powered by a rechargeable micro protonic battery. The battery provides sufficient energy for several days of continuous navigation operation.

Although the system is designed for extended independent operation, its energy reserves are finite. During prolonged terrestrial operations, the pilot must eventually locate compatible replacement batteries or establish access to suitable charging equipment.

The helmet's electronic architecture supports additional capabilities, which may be introduced as Kara's equipment and available infrastructure develop.

### Established capabilities

- Independent terrestrial navigation.
- Precise position reporting.
- Directional heading.
- Elevation reporting.
- Integrated heads-up display.
- Rechargeable micro protonic battery.

### Undeveloped capabilities

- Battery replacement.
- Battery charging.
- Navigation waypoint storage.
- Terrain mapping.
- Environmental sensing.
- Communications integration.
- Equipment diagnostics.

The underlying physics of protonic energy storage, battery capacity, charging requirements, and planetary positioning technology remain to be established.
