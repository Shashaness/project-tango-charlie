# TC167

## Canon

**TC167** is the designation of [Commander Kara Voss's](../characters/kara_voss.md) assigned fighter. It sustained damage during atmospheric entry on [Earth](../locations/earth.md) and crashed near an airport after Kara lost consciousness. Kara survived. The aircraft is severely damaged and may be beyond repair. [Initial world state](../world/initial_world_state.md)

## Proposed

The [first chapter](../chronicles/001_the_return.md) proposes adaptations for reconnaissance and surface operations, plus turbulence and cascading failures during entry. [Kara's first journal entry](../journals/kara_voss/001_first_entry.md) proposes reports about system response and a receiver event. These are draft details.

## Pending development

The specifics of atmospheric entry, the extent of the damage, and the aircraft's technical specifications await approval. Future technology references may draw on a review of the game implementation.
