# Initial world state

## Canon

During uncontrolled descent, Kara sees a city and attempts to reach an airport near it. She loses consciousness before TC167 crashes near the airbase. She survives. The aircraft is severely damaged and its prospects for repair are uncertain. Kara must reach the maintenance hangar and recover it. The Jeep is at the outlying service compound, and the recovery trolley is at the airport. [Canon](../CANON.md#q1-additions)
