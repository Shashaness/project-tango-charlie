# Abandoned airport

## Canon

Kara saw the airport near a city during her descent and attempted to reach it. After awakening at the crash site, she saw several standing buildings and set out toward the maintenance hangar. The outlying service compound holds a utility Jeep. The airport contains a maintenance hangar and a heavy recovery hangar. [Chapter II](../chronicles/002_the_arrival.md) · [Canon](../CANON.md)

## Character knowledge

The airbase appears abandoned. Kara learns what is accessible and what equipment remains through exploration in [Q001](../missions/Q001_remnants_of_civilization.md). The airport and city remain unnamed.

## Playable layout

The Arrival world places the service compound apart from the airport and connects them by a paved service route. The airport retains its runway and taxiways and has two accessible hangars. Their local placements and paths are gameplay layout. The identity and history of the people who ran the airport remain open.
