# Earth

## Canon

Earth is humanity's ancestral home and the destination of the return expedition. Centuries of environmental abuse and nuclear, chemical, and biological warfare transformed it into a largely uninhabitable desert wasteland with a surface resembling Mars. It retains an atmosphere, substantially intact gravity, and enough atmospheric oxygen to be somewhat breathable. Earthquakes and volcanic activity are becoming more frequent.

[Commander Kara Voss](../characters/kara_voss.md) is tasked with investigating these conditions, establishing a permanent operating base and survival infrastructure, and assessing the feasibility of restoration.

## In-universe belief

Humanity is generally believed to have abandoned Earth. Kara believes she is alone.

## Unresolved

The oxygen source, cause of increasing geological instability, presence of inhabitants, state of surviving infrastructure, and feasibility of restoration remain open. [Mystery register](../MYSTERIES.md)
