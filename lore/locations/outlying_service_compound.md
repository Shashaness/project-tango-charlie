# Outlying service compound

A small facility stands apart from the airport near the [crash site](crash_site.md). Its former purpose appears to have involved utilities or maintenance. The identities of its operators and the reasons they left remain open.

The compound's garage holds a [Jeep](../technology/jeep.md). Kara learns its location by exploring. Four structures and a paved service route establish the playable space. [Q001](../missions/Q001_remnants_of_civilization.md)
