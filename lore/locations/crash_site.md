# TC167 crash site

Kara lost consciousness before impact and awoke in the wreck near the airport. The aircraft is severely damaged. She set out toward the standing airport buildings. An outlying facility offers a nearer place to look for transportation. [Chapter II](../chronicles/002_the_arrival.md) · [Outlying service compound](outlying_service_compound.md)
