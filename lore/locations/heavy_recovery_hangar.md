# Heavy recovery hangar

The second hangar houses a [stationary crane](../technology/crane_system.md) and [recovery trolley](../technology/recovery_trolley.md). Kara can use the key from the maintenance office to unlock the personnel door. The interior control operates the larger vehicle gate in the playable scenario. Its source of power remains open.

“Hangar B” is a development identifier. [Q002](../missions/Q002_means_of_recovery.md)
