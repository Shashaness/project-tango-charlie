# Kara Voss — conditional mission entries

Each entry is recorded after its associated gameplay event.

## Finding the compound

> There are several buildings between the crash site and the airport. They look like some kind of service facility. I can't see any movement, but the structures appear to be reasonably intact. It's worth investigating before I attempt the journey to the airbase.

## Driving toward the airport

> The Jeep works. I still can't quite believe it. As I drive toward the airport, the city is becoming clearer. The buildings are enormous. I've seen cities like this in the archives, but standing here, seeing one with my own eyes, is something entirely different.

## Discovering the office

> There's an office inside the hangar. A desk, a computer, even a printer. Whoever worked here left behind the ordinary things that made up their daily lives. I wonder if they knew they would never return.

## Collecting the key

> I found a key inside one of the desk drawers. I should see what it opens.

## Finding the recovery equipment

> This is what I was hoping to find. Heavy lifting equipment, recovery machinery, and enough space to work on something the size of TC167. If I can get these systems operational, I might actually have a chance.

If Kara has already tried the locked personnel door:

> I found a key inside one of the desk drawers. There's another hangar nearby, but its personnel entrance is locked. Perhaps this belongs to it.
