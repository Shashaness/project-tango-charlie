# Commander Kara Voss's journal

The journal records observations, decisions, discoveries, and personal experiences from [Kara's](../../characters/kara_voss.md) perspective. A numbered entry carries a title, location, mission day, and entry number. Future simulation-generated entries should trace back to actual events and reflect what Kara could know at the time. Publication requires review of private observations.

[Entry 001](001_first_entry.md) is **PROPOSED** and written as an in-universe account. The relative mission-day label, extent of damage, responding systems, quantity of supplies, oxygen readings, and brief receiver event await approval. Kara's suggestion of atmospheric interference is her interpretation. The entry leaves the signal's origin and [other mysteries](../../MYSTERIES.md) open.

[Mission entry templates](mission_entries.md) record conditional text for Q001. The campaign adds the entries to the local save when Kara encounters the associated events.
