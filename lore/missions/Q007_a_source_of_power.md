# Q007 — A Source of Power

**Status:** PLANNED  
**Classification:** Personal equipment and infrastructure  
**Assigned character:** Commander Kara Voss

Kara needs equipment capable of recharging micro protonic batteries. Locations, activation thresholds, and campaign dependencies remain open.

### Proposed objectives

1. Identify compatible charging equipment.
2. Locate it and inspect its condition.
3. Establish a suitable power source.
4. Restore charging functionality if necessary.
5. Recharge a depleted battery.

Future systems include electrical power infrastructure, equipment restoration, battery charging, and persistent battery state.
