# Q001 — Remnants of Civilization

**Main campaign. Owner:** Commander Kara Voss. **Prerequisite:** Arrival begins.

Kara seeks transportation before attempting the journey to the airport. The route uses the [outlying service compound](../locations/outlying_service_compound.md), its Jeep, and the airport's [maintenance hangar](../locations/maintenance_hangar.md).

1. Locate the compound and explore its structures.
2. Discover, inspect, and board the Jeep.
3. Demonstrate powered driving.
4. Reach the airport and enter the maintenance hangar.

The Jeep must be discovered and driven before Q001 completes. Kara may reach the airport on foot first, and valid early observations carry forward. Completion opens [Q002](Q002_means_of_recovery.md). See [conditional journal entries](../journals/kara_voss/mission_entries.md) and the [quest definitions](../../assets/quests/arrival.json).
