# Commander Kara Voss

## Canon

Kara is the first canonical PTC character. She originates from a human colony that departed Earth long ago and knows Earth primarily through historical archives. Her colony dispatched her to investigate whether humanity can return. She survived atmospheric entry in her assigned fighter, [TC167](../technology/tc167.md), which sustained damage and requires repairs.

Her objectives are to establish a permanent operating base, assess habitability, investigate Earth's environmental conditions, build infrastructure for long-term survival, and determine whether terraforming and restoration can make Earth capable of supporting civilization. She is there to help restore humanity's ancestral home.

## Proposed characterization

Kara is envisioned as resourceful, independent, intelligent, disciplined, scientifically curious, technically capable, pragmatic, compassionate, and willing to take initiative. Her commitment to human survival and Earth's restoration guides this direction. Her intended role is a persistent character with her own memories, responsibilities, objectives, experiences, and relationships.

## In-universe belief

Kara believes humanity abandoned [Earth](../locations/earth.md) and that she is alone. See the [mystery register](../MYSTERIES.md).

## Unresolved or pending development

Kara's childhood, education, military history, age, appearance, relationships, and deeper personality remain open, along with the circumstances of entry, extent of damage, and initial resources. Read the proposed [first chapter](../chronicles/001_the_return.md) and [journal entry](../journals/kara_voss/001_first_entry.md) as drafts.

## Personal equipment

Kara operates the [Terrestrial Navigation Helmet](../technology/terrestrial_navigation_helmet.md). Its navigation electronics run independently of TC167.
